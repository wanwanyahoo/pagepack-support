PagePack

Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

On-device processing

PagePack processes selected photos, image files, and document scans on your device. The developer does not receive your photos, scans, PDFs, or file names. PagePack has no account, advertising, or analytics SDK.

Camera, photos, and files

Camera access is used when you choose to scan a document. The system photo picker shares only the images you select. PagePack keeps temporary working copies to build and preview PDFs. Save a copy to Files or share it to keep the finished PDF. Destinations you choose are managed by their own providers and privacy settings.

Preferences and support

Your selected interface language is stored on the device. If you post on the public support tracker, GitHub handles that information according to its privacy statement. You can reach the developer through the PagePack support tracker.
