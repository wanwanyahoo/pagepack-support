PagePack

Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

On-device processing

PagePack processes selected photos, image files, and document scans on your device. The developer does not receive your photos, scans, PDFs, or file names. PagePack has no account, advertising, or analytics SDK.

Camera, photos, and files

Camera access is used when you choose to scan a document. The system photo picker shares only the images you select. PagePack keeps temporary working copies to process photos and build PDFs. When you choose Save to Photos, add-only photo permission is used to save exported copies. Original photos are not changed. Exported photo copies do not copy source location, capture date or camera metadata; visible private details require your review. Save results to Photos or Files, or share them, to keep them. Destinations you choose are managed by their own providers and privacy settings.

Preferences and support

Your selected interface language and any photo-processing templates you save are stored on the device. Templates contain settings, not photos. Verification reports are generated locally and are shared only when you choose a destination. If you post on the public support tracker, GitHub handles that information according to its privacy statement. You can reach the developer through the PagePack support tracker.
