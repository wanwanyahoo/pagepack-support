PagePack Support

Combine photos and paper scans into one PDF on your iPhone or iPad.

Make a PDF

  1. Add photos from your library or Files. Tap Scan to capture paper pages on a device with a supported camera.
  2. Drag the handles to reorder pages. Tap a page for a closer look, or open its menu to rotate, move, or remove it.
  3. Choose A4, US Letter, or Match photo. Set the upload limit and open PDF options to choose a file name or change the margins.
  4. Tap Create PDF, review the actual file size, then save to Files or share it.

If your upload limit is too small

PagePack reduces image resolution and compression quality in steps to meet your limit. If the PDF still exceeds the limit, choose a larger limit or remove pages.

Get help

Report a problem on the PagePack support tracker. Include your app version, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps to reproduce. Keep photos and private documents out of public reports.
