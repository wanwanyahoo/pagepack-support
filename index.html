PagePack Support

Ten photo tools, from a single upload copy to a complete delivery set. Compress, crop, resize, convert, adjust color, add text watermarks, cover private details, make collages, build PDFs and recheck delivered files. Processing is on your iPhone or iPad, free, without an account, ads or purchases.

Prepare a photo batch

  1. Open Photo tools and choose an upload, resize, conversion, color, watermark or privacy task. Add several still images from Photos or Files, or try the bundled sample photos.
  2. Choose a format, optional KB limit and sizing mode. Edit each photo using crop or its menu: rotate, flip, inspect details or cover private content.
  3. Export & verify, then inspect the actual bytes, pixels, format and metadata checks. Share a per-file verification report if needed.
  4. Save copies to Photos or Files, or share them. Save a template to reuse the settings. Original files remain unchanged.

Still images only, up to 4096 pixels per side. JPG and HEIC are lossy. Exact canvas modes fail rather than silently changing dimensions if a byte limit cannot be met. Verification checks file settings, not a website's identity or photo-content rules.

Prepare and recheck a delivery set

  1. Open Delivery set, add up to 50 still photos, and set up to six versions per photo. Each version has its own dimensions, format, optional KB cap and optional text watermark.
  2. Export the photos together with the PDF contact sheet, CSV results and JSON delivery record. Failed exports remain listed; the contact sheet uses actual exported copies.
  3. Open Check delivery from the home list. Choose the saved JSON record, add the delivered photos from Files and tap Check delivery. The app rereads actual bytes, pixels, format, listed private metadata and SHA-256 digests.
  4. Inspect missing, changed, renamed, duplicate and unlisted files. Matching a record is a file comparison, not proof of authorship, authenticity or acceptance by a website.

Save to Files initially opens a new batch folder inside PagePack’s Exports folder on your device. You can choose another destination. Saved copies remain until you remove them in Files; temporary working copies are separate. A separate folder keeps repeated exports together; choosing another existing destination can still create name collisions.

Combine photos

Collage & combine supports up to nine still images, two- or three-column grids, horizontal and vertical strips, spacing, ordering and individual crops. The whole collage has a longest side of 2048 pixels. This tool does not automatically remove overlapping screenshot regions.

Make a PDF

  1. Add photos from your library or Files. Tap Scan to capture paper pages on a device with a supported camera.
  2. Drag the handles to reorder pages. Tap a page for a closer look, or open its menu to rotate, move, or remove it.
  3. Choose A4, US Letter, or Match photo. Set the upload limit and open PDF options to choose a file name or change the margins.
  4. Tap Create PDF, review the actual file size, then save to Files or share it.

If your upload limit is too small

PagePack reduces image resolution and compression quality in steps to meet your limit. If the PDF still exceeds the limit, choose a larger limit or remove pages.

Get help

Report a problem on the PagePack support tracker. Include your app version, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps to reproduce. Keep photos and private documents out of public reports.
